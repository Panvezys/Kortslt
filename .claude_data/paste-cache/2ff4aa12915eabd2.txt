Let's look at a critical edge case that this change introduces to make sure we don't accidentally leave a vulnerability in your calendar logic:

The "Double-Booking" Hazard (Independent vs. Attached)
Now that a coach has two types of services—some that automatically tie to a platform court (floating/court-bound) and some that skip the court entirely (independent)—the coach's calendar engine must handle conflicts defensively.

Imagine a coach is booked for an Independent Service on Tuesday at 14:00.

The Good: The coach's calendar is locked for that time slot.

The Danger: Because an independent service doesn't lock a platform court, a standard player could navigate to a court page and try to book that same platform court for Tuesday at 14:00. This is perfectly fine because the court is free.

The Conflict: However, if a second student tries to book that same coach at that same court for Tuesday at 14:00 via a floating service, the system must recognize that while the court is free, the coach is already busy coaching an independent lesson somewhere else.

The Improvement Check
Ensure that your POST /bookings verification logic checks calendar availability in the following order:

Coach Check: If the requested booking includes a coachId, the system must query all bookings for that coach at that time block—regardless of whether those existing bookings have a courtId or are independent. If the coach is busy, fail immediately.

Court Check: If the requested booking is not independent (i.e., it needs a platform court), the system then checks if that specific courtId is free.

If your assistant already covered this global coachId check during Phase 2's validation updates, you are in the clear!
