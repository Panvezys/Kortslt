. The "Double Vision" Problem
Look at the space between the green summary box and the final checkout button. You are repeating three pieces of information right next to each other:

The Policy: You have "Taikoma standartinė atšaukimo politika" inside the box, and then immediately below it in the footer, you repeat "Taikoma standartinė atšaukimo politika (iki 80% grąžinimas)."

The Price: The summary box highlights 112.00 €, and the footer right below it says Serija iš viso: 112.00 €.

The Time/Date: The box says "08:00 – 10:00", and the footer repeats "an, geg. 26 · 08:00 - 10:00 · 2...".

2. The Cluttered Sticky Footer
The sticky footer should be the cleanest, most undeniable part of the UI. Right now, it is trying to hold too much text, which is causing the date/time string to truncate (2...) and look broken. Furthermore, you have two trash can icons—one at the top ("Išvalyti") and one at the bottom next to the date. This makes it confusing for the user to know which one clears the form.

3. The Chat Bubble Overlap (Critical)
Notice the floating green chat bubble in the bottom right corner? It is physically overlapping your "4x Rezervuoti" button. On mobile devices, an overlapping floating action button (FAB) can completely prevent users from clicking "checkout."

The Solution: Ruthless Simplification
We need to strip the sticky footer down to its absolute essentials: Total Price and the Action Button. Everything else belongs inside the scrollable summary box above it.
