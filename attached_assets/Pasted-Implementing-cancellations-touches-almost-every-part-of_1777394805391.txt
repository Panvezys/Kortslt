Implementing cancellations touches almost every part of your app. Here is exactly what happens behind the scenes and what else you need to prepare.

A. The "My Bookings" Page (User View)
Visual Status Badges: Instead of just showing the date and time, bookings need clear status labels like [Confirmed], [Cancelled - 50% Refunded], or [Cancelled - No Refund].

Historical Accuracy: Cancelled bookings should not disappear from the user's history. They should remain visible so the user has a record of the transaction and the refund.

B. Notifications (Emails/SMS)
You will need to set up two types of automated emails when a cancellation happens:

To the User (The Receipt): * Subject: "Your Booking Cancellation & Refund Info - Korts.lt"

Body: Confirm the slot is cancelled. State the exact amount refunded (e.g., "€10.00 has been refunded to your original payment method. Please allow 5-10 business days for it to appear on your statement").

To the Court Owner/Admin: * Subject: "Slot Opened: Cancellation on [Date]"

Body: "A booking for [Date/Time] has been cancelled. The court is now marked as available on the public calendar. You retained €X.XX from the cancellation fee."

C. Where to display the Rules (Legal & Trust)
To completely protect yourself from Stripe Chargebacks, the policy must be visible in three places:

Checkout Page: (Covered in the prompt above). Just a single sentence near the "Pay" button.

Confirmation Email: When they first book the court, the email receipt must have a "Cancellation Policy" footer.

Terms of Service Page (/terms): You need a dedicated paragraph detailing the 48h/24h breakdown and explaining that Stripe processing times may delay the funds reaching their bank.

D. The Owner's Dashboard (Admin View)
Your admin panel needs a significant update to track this, otherwise your accounting will be a mess.

Financial Metrics: Your dashboard must distinguish between Gross Revenue (total money taken) and Net Revenue (money kept after refunds and Stripe fees).

Calendar View: The admin calendar needs to visually show that a slot was cancelled but is now open again (perhaps a hollow box instead of a solid green booked box).

Manual Override: As the owner, you need a "Force Cancel & Full Refund" button on your dashboard. Sometimes users have legitimate emergencies (injury, extreme weather), and you might want to bypass your own 48h rule to provide good customer service.
