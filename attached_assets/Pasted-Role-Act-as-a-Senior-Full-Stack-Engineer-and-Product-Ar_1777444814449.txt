Role: Act as a Senior Full-Stack Engineer and Product Architect.

Objective: Consolidate our "Coaching" and "Training" systems into a single, professional "Coaches Marketplace" and build a bidirectional approval workflow between Coaches and Facility Owners.

Part 1: Cleanup & Consolidation

Delete the Trainer System: Completely remove the trainers table and all associated logic. Delete the following files: lib/db/src/schema/trainers.ts, artifacts/api-server/src/routes/trainers.ts, artifacts/courtbook/src/pages/trainers.tsx, and trainer-detail.tsx.

Standardize on Coaches: We will exclusively use the coachesTable, courtCoachesTable, and courtCoachInvitationsTable architecture.

Part 2: Bidirectional Approval Workflow (Logic)

Update courtCoachInvitationsTable: Ensure the initiated_by column supports both 'owner' (Facility inviting a Coach) and 'coach' (Coach applying to a Facility).

Endpoint - Coach Requests: Create a POST route /api/coaches/apply-to-facility where a Coach can search for a facility and send a request to teach there.

Endpoint - Owner Response: Create a POST route /api/owner/respond-to-coach where a Facility Owner can ACCEPT or REJECT a pending coach request.

Automation: If a request is ACCEPTED, automatically insert a record into courtCoachesTable to link them.

Part 3: The Coach Experience (UI)

Coach Dashboard Update: On the Coach’s private profile/dashboard, add a 'My Facilities' section.

Facility Search: Add a search bar or list of available Facilities (from the facilities table). Allow the Coach to click 'Apply to teach here'.

Status Tracking: Show the Coach their pending applications and which facilities they are currently 'Active' at.

Part 4: The Owner Experience (UI)

Manage Staff Tab: In the Owner Dashboard, add a 'Coaches & Staff' tab.

Pending Requests: Display a list of Coaches waiting for approval to work at their courts. Show the Coach’s bio, sports, and price.

Roster Management: Display a list of already approved Coaches. Provide a 'Remove' button to unlink a coach if they no longer work there.

Part 5: The Public Marketplace (Frontend)

Coach Profile Page (coach.tsx): Update the profile to dynamically list every Facility where that coach is approved to teach.

Facility Page Integration: On the individual Court/Facility page, add a 'Available Coaches' section that shows the profiles of coaches linked to that specific court.

Professional UI: Enhance the coaches.tsx search page with filters for Sport and City. Ensure the cards look premium (showing the coach's badge, price, and primary sports).

Part 6: Roles & Permissions

Ensure that only users with the coach role can apply to facilities.

Ensure that only users with the owner role can approve coaches for their specific facilities.

Ensure the 'Become a Coach' flow (become-coach.tsx) correctly updates the user's role in the database/Clerk upon approval.
